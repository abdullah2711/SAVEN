## 1 Commercial position

Sell a verified reduction in the effort of running dependable accounts and connected operations. The buyer should see how SAVEN improves the first migration, the next invoice, the next stock issue, the next approval, and the next close. AI is part of the delivery mechanism and can be demonstrated, but it is not evidence of the outcome by itself.

The proposed positioning is an accounting and finance platform with connected business functions and an assistant that works through the same controlled tools as the staff. Shared SaaS is the primary delivery model. Private deployment is a separately costed premium engagement, not a cheap way to avoid the shared service.

The assertion that incumbents do not offer AI is already false: QuickBooks publishes accounting and finance AI capabilities. Do not advertise SAVEN as the only AI accounting product, the first AI, unbeatable, breach-proof, or universally capable. Existing products, local vendors, accountants, spreadsheets, and doing nothing are all competitors. The product must win specific tasks under comparable conditions. [R1]

Industry eligibility remains broad. Initial acquisition experiments should concentrate effort without excluding other qualified customers. Test professional services, trading and distribution, and project businesses as separate cohorts; include manufacturing and HR-intensive design partners to validate the requested suite. Do not accept a production obligation merely because a design partner volunteers.

## 3 Qualification and fit boundaries

Discovery starts with the customer's last difficult task. Ask them to show one invoice, one stock or asset problem, one approval, one recurring report, and the most recent migration or close issue. Record frequency, people involved, time, corrections, consequence of failure, and evidence available. Obtain permission before using sample documents and anonymize public demonstrations.

Qualify transaction peaks, legal companies, payroll arrangements, manufacturing routes, statutory reporting, connectivity, migration quality, and required integrations. Classify each requirement as supported and proven, supported but awaiting validation, configuration work, extension work, or outside the current contract. A signed fit checklist prevents the promise of a patient system, securities back office, unlimited manufacturing, or statutory coverage that has not been built.

The sales process advances from qualified need to a representative demonstration, then to agreed pilot measures, commercial terms, migration rehearsal, and go-live acceptance. A lead is not won when the owner likes a dashboard. It is won when the users can complete their work and the buyer accepts the resulting cost and controls.

## 5 Demonstration and conversation scripts

### The short owner conversation

Ask where the owner currently gets the cash position and what they must do when they cannot trust it. Show that answer in SAVEN, then drill to the invoice, receipt, journal, and evidence. Show the exception queue and one approval. Explain that staff still own review and business decisions. End with a proposed pilot based on their own recurring tasks and an agreed comparison, rather than a tour of every menu.

### The accountant demonstration

Use one clear supplier invoice, one ambiguous document, and one duplicate scan. Extract the clear invoice, show the source regions and draft treatment, and submit through approval. The ambiguous document must stop for missing information; the duplicate must not create another liability. Open the resulting ledger and supplier balance. Demonstrating a safe refusal is stronger evidence than hiding the difficult case.

### The storekeeper demonstration

Give an unfamiliar user a device and a simple task: issue one box from the permitted store to a department. Observe without coaching. Repeat with a barcode, insufficient stock, an interrupted submission, and a required approval. The task succeeds only if the user understands whether stock has actually left and whether the transaction posted. Do not make the user converse with AI for a frequent simple issue.

### The finance and reporting demonstration

Open a cash forecast with visible actuals, commitments, and assumptions. Change collection timing and show the effect, then restore the original scenario. Open a professional statement pack, drill from a numerical note to its schedule and ledger, and show a narrative note awaiting evidence. This demonstrates financial work inside the system without pretending software can infer facts the customer has not supplied.

### The administrator demonstration

Describe an approval threshold, inspect the proposed rule, simulate a transaction above and below the limit, publish the version, and inspect the in-flight policy. Then attempt to retire Inventory while Manufacturing depends on it. The system must explain the dependency and transition route. This is the direct answer to the user's module-conflict experience.

### The company knowledge demonstration

Ask a business-context question, open the answer's supporting contract or policy at the cited clause and version, then inspect the history after an approved source change. Rename the file and verify that the citation still resolves correctly. Show a private fact staying private, a missing-source answer explaining its gap, and a company administrator disabling the feature. Position this as retained, traceable business knowledge from authorized information; do not claim the assistant knows everything about the company or treats remembered statements as approval authority.

## 6 Objections and responsible responses

| Objection | Response to use | Required proof or next action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Excel already works | Compare one recurring task and its hidden review cost | Agree a timed baseline |
| Our staff cannot learn another ERP | Let an unfamiliar user complete the real task | Record success and help required |
| AI will post the wrong thing | Explain drafts, evidence, authority and approvals | Show ambiguity and invalid action tests |
| Your team can see our information | Explain normal access and exceptional support access honestly | Access policy and customer-visible history |
| What if you are hacked | Explain tested controls, incident handling and recovery limits | Assessment and restore evidence |
| We need private deployment | Establish legal, network, recovery and operating needs first | Costed support and responsibility matrix |
| We need this special process | Walk through its inputs, states and accounting effects | Fit checklist and acceptance case |
| Another vendor is cheaper | Compare implementation, support, AI and staff time | Written total-cost comparison |
| We do not want to lose our data | Show complete export and contract rights | Independently reconcile a sample export |
| Will you replace thirty people | Measure tasks and retained duties before a staffing decision | Pilot workload and quality evidence |
| Does this guarantee compliance | State the exact validated country pack and integration scope | Current applicability and test evidence |

Never attack staff competence or describe customers as uneducated in sales material. Their reluctance often reflects a rational concern about disruption, dependency, and cost. The product must earn confidence through observed ease and dependable operation.

## 7 Pricing and unit economics

No market price is finalized. Test package hypotheses based on company count, active users, operating depth, transaction volume, storage, support, and AI workload. Keep the basic accounting workflow affordable and explain limits before purchase. Avoid charging for every click or making a customer's lawful evidence inaccessible because of a surprise overage.

Consider a small-business package, a growing-team package, and a controlled-operations package, with clearly listed entitlements. Manufacturing, POS and HR remain product capabilities; packaging determines access, not whether they exist in the release. Private deployment includes implementation, annual maintenance, infrastructure responsibility, upgrade support, and a minimum commercially viable contract.

Track monthly recurring revenue separately from implementation income. Contribution equals subscription revenue less hosting, storage, AI, direct support, payment handling, and approved referral acquisition cost. Gross margin definitions must be consistent. Acquisition payback uses acquisition spend divided by monthly contribution from acquired customers, and must account for churn and collection delays.

For illustration only, PKR 10,000 monthly revenue less PKR 1,200 hosting and storage, PKR 800 AI, PKR 1,500 direct support, and PKR 1,000 approved referral acquisition cost leaves PKR 5,500 contribution, or 55 percent before fixed operating costs. These are invented planning inputs, not market prices or cost estimates. If acquisition cost were PKR 30,000, simple payback would be about 5.5 months before churn and financing effects. Measure actual cohort costs before setting a durable price.

Manual bank-transfer verification is the initial payment workflow. An application submission records package, billing details, evidence and status. Operators verify funds, record a receipt, approve entitlements, and provision. Renewals have due dates, reminders, grace, and documented restricted-access behaviour. Automatic payment processing is a later integration, with no current claim that it exists.

## 8 Channels and marketing execution

Begin with founder-led discovery and demonstrations using Economic Systems Consulting's existing market knowledge, subject to client confidentiality and professional independence. Do not imply an audit opinion endorses the product. Consultants can introduce or implement SAVEN, but responsibilities, commissions, customer ownership, data access, support, and conflicts must be explicit.

Authorized referral and implementation assistance includes a sandbox, task certification based on outcomes, implementation checklist, escalation path, and renewal economics. A referral partner receives no access to client records unless the client grants an appropriate role. Multi-client accountant workspaces keep company context explicit and prevent cross-client AI memory.

Use task-focused demonstrations, short evidence-backed case studies, migration guides, accountant workshops, and narrowly targeted outreach. Initial content topics are issuing stock without account selection, avoiding duplicate asset openings, approval changes without code, professional statements with notes, and cash forecasting from controlled records. Advertising should lead to one relevant demonstration and clear fit criteria.

Run small acquisition experiments before broad paid campaigns. Track qualified conversations, demonstrations, pilot starts, first verified opening position, paid conversions, retained active use, support demand, and contribution. Attribution must distinguish founder relationships from repeatable channels. Stop or redesign a campaign when it attracts poor-fit customers or produces negative contribution despite registrations.

## 9 Defensibility and the next decade

Code and AI access alone are weak defenses. A first-mover story, SaaS delivery, attractive glass effects, or a long feature list can be copied. Build a small number of reinforcing advantages with evidence, rather than claiming hundreds of independent moats.

| Potential advantage | How it compounds | Metric | Main weakness |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Reliable migration and configuration methods | More proven templates reduce future setup effort | Successful cutover time and exceptions | Competitors can copy visible templates |
| Trusted accountants and implementation partners | Local expertise and referrals lower acquisition cost | Partner retention and contribution | Poor partner work damages trust |
| Correct connected workflows | Repeated reconciliation evidence builds operating confidence | Error and correction trends | One serious failure can erase trust |
| Domain evaluation suite | New AI models can be assessed quickly against real tasks | Cost at equal accepted quality | Private cases need rights and careful curation |
| Useful integration ecosystem | More working connectors reduce customer effort | Active reliable integrations | External APIs and platforms change |
| Customer-informed usability | Observed task improvements accumulate | First-use success and time saved | Feedback can drive unmaintainable customization |
| Transparent operations and exit | Trust reduces adoption resistance | Retention and security review success | Easy exit removes coercive retention |

Customer data is not a free training asset. Reusable knowledge should come from permitted anonymized patterns, public requirements, synthetic tests, and explicit agreements. Preserve data rights while improving templates and evaluations. A proprietary benchmark is useful only if it predicts customer outcomes and is continually refreshed.

Expand geographically through country packs, reporting and employment validation, partner capability, pricing evidence, data-processing terms, and regional performance tests. Do not turn on a new currency and announce country compliance. A ten-year view should shape adaptable contracts and capital discipline; forecasts of unassailable dominance are not a strategy.

## 10 Initial commercial experiments and stop rules

The first research cycle recruits a small set of consenting design partners across the priority acquisition cohorts, plus at least one manufacturing and one payroll-intensive case. Establish baseline task time, reviewer effort, material errors, close duration, and support costs before showing automation. Internal prototype results do not count as customer production evidence.

Proposed pilot gates are at least 30 percent lower median human time on the selected repeatable tasks, no deterioration in material error outcomes, at least 80 percent first-use success on the defined simple tasks, and credible positive contribution at the proposed package. These are test targets, not advertising claims. Record results by cohort and task; a blended average must not hide a failed workflow.

Pause onboarding when financial reconciliation fails, unauthorized access is observed, material production defects remain unresolved, customer requirements exceed supported depth, or implementation costs cannot fit the proposed commercial model. Fixing those issues before scaling protects both customer and business.
