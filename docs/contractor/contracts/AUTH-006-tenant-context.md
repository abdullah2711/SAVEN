DESIGN: use one fixed, documented PostgreSQL transaction-scoped authority-gate advisory key. Admission acquires the SHARED gate before taking authority row locks or reading the membership/role set, then holds it on the auth transaction until the business transaction ends. INSERT/UPDATE/DELETE statements on auth.tenant_membership and auth.roles acquire the same gate EXCLUSIVELY through BEFORE STATEMENT triggers, before their row writes; this covers previously absent/new associations and global assurance-role changes. Membership and role writers share this single gate, avoiding the proposal's user-key/role-key inversion across mixed writer transactions. Do not implement its claim that no writer can take roles then user merely because each row trigger takes one key. The gate is infrastructural, not a configurable business rule or hardcoded role domain. Concurrent admissions may share it; authority writes serialize against all admitted business work. Document this deliberate global authority-write contention and later extension options without a throughput/SLA claim.
