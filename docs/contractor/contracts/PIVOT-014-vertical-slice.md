## Design and counting contract

Expose a reusable, versioned application Request/Response composition through injected auth, tenant-context and persistence ports; this is the actual PG application's request handler, not a test-only imitation of one. Compose the accepted auth adapter and AUTH-006 transaction boundary. Login, session verification and selected membership are genuine; no test principal/header or direct service invocation substitutes for request admission. Preserve exact-origin/CSRF requirements. Runtime uses actual restricted auth/customer logins, never administrator connections or SET ROLE from an owner connection. Auth authority remains reserved until business transaction completion on the one owned business client; retain the documented distinction between auth and business commits and safe uncertainty handling.

Implement only a plain, single-company, single-currency journal with two decimal-balanced lines and authorized direct posting. Persist creation as a draft/source distinct from POSTED; post and void are separate durable transitions. Use the accepted canonical source/policy/permission shape and accepted 042 terminal-void primitive and projections; no bespoke alternative void implementation. Resolve module/company/resource and separate post/delete/read permissions from verified authority, never role-name hardcoding. Refuse unsupported linked trade/stock cases explicitly. Chart/accounts, periods, company and references are synthetic tenant data, never constants granting authority. Reference-domain extension remains data-driven.

Audit counts are explicit: `tenant_data.audit` is the canonical logical command/lifecycle stream; `tenant_data.audit_event` retains the accepted per-physical-row change stream, and auth/security audit remains independent. Exactly one canonical lifecycle event is required per successful create, post and void mutation; exactly one change event is required for each physical mutation covered by 041, with an explicit expected-row inventory for the operation. A multi-row journal is not permission to drop line audit or count a filtered subset as the entire audit trail. Tests assert both streams independently and enumerate all operation-correlated rows, including any accepted 042 explicit event, without duplicate emission. The cover sheet and delivery disclose these denominators. Do not suppress 041 triggers, merge streams or change applied payload bytes to manufacture a count of one. If accepted source contracts make these exact counts impossible, stop for Principal clarification rather than weakening them.

Use verified actor and tenant and truthful application origin/request correlation on every event. Before/after serialization must follow accepted schema-aware redaction; use synthetic secret sentinels and assert absence recursively in all audit payloads and outward responses. Raw session tokens never appear in UI/API/list payloads; only protected HttpOnly cookie transport carries the credential. Count operation effects from captured baselines after login/setup so pre-existing authentication rows cannot conceal or inflate business mutation counts.

Number allocation is advance-only, serialized with a scoped row lock and a non-reusable sequence identity. No transactional rollback may rewind an identity already allocated, including an allocation made by a later failed post. Preserve permanent allocation/cancellation evidence and immutable party/transaction binding; no deletion/recycling/renumbering or MAX+1. Reinstallation/reapplication must validate supported allocator profile and retain the largest issued boundary, including retained bindings/receipts and external recovery evidence; exhaustion refuses. Any needed 043 must be independently atomic for transactional objects, gate-first, with narrow offline ownership, no BYPASSRLS or new customer cross-tenant authority, and fail-closed validated installation. Nontransactional sequence advancement is deliberately not rolled back. Validate an unbalanced request inside the business transaction before allocation; it must leave no business journal, line or audit trace. Document-number gaps are distinct from duplicate identities.

- **S1 — REAL APPLICATION PATH:** Every tested create/post/read/void/audit request enters the real versioned application handler with a real session, passes the accepted tenant gate and executes the real service/repository on its owned client. Setup SQL may provision synthetic identities, memberships, reference/chart/company/period data and schema only; it may not insert target journals, journal lines, lifecycle receipts or target audit evidence. Instrumentation demonstrates the request→session→gate→service path and actual restricted database principal. A raw-SQL lifecycle fixture cannot satisfy this property.
- **S2 — PERMANENT AUTOMATED TEST:** `tests/pivot-014-vertical-slice.test.mjs` is committed and actually runs inside `scripts/check.mjs` on every invocation, against an automatically created disposable local PostgreSQL database/cluster with automatic cleanup, deterministic identities/clocks and bounded synchronization. No manual preparation, live credentials, SSH, cloud, shared production database or environment skip can yield success. Missing local prerequisites fail nonzero with an actionable error; no silent skip/fallback. Preserve existing check behavior and avoid recursive check invocation. Mutation/failure cleanup leaves no owned clients, cluster or temporary credentials behind. **NO-FLAKINESS:** This universal gate must be flake-proof: no retry loop may mask an intermittent failure; establish and enforce a bounded wall-clock budget for the whole lifecycle test, deterministic disposable-database port allocation and deterministic cleanup. Every transient, including connection teardown, timeout or port collision, is reported as a failure to diagnose, never retried to green; exhausting the whole-test budget fails the gate and still performs bounded cleanup. Declare the chosen whole-test budget and allocation/cleanup protocol in delivery evidence. A flaky universal gate is unacceptable because retrying it conceals defects.
- **S3 — POSITIVELY ASSERTED CROSS-TENANT REFUSAL:** Provision two tenants with books and create their journals through application requests. A valid tenant-A session requesting tenant-B's journal must receive the specific tenant/resource authorization error at the application layer; independently the same session requesting B's audit history receives the specific audit-authorization refusal. Assert no foreign journal/line/audit disclosure or mutation, not merely empty RLS results. Pair each refusal with the same session's permitted own-journal/own-audit success and tenant B's authorized success; audit-layer isolation must be proved separately from journal isolation. Record required denial evidence through the accepted security-audit boundary without fabricating a successful B business mutation or leaking B payloads.
- **S4 — BALANCED POSTS; UNBALANCED LEAVES NO TRACE:** A balanced request commits exact equal decimal debits/credits and a POSTED journal. An otherwise equivalent unbalanced create-and-post request reaches transaction validation and fails with the specific unbalanced-entry error. Query independent persisted-state baselines after completion and assert NO journal, NO journal line and NO business audit row in either stream survived for that request; no hidden draft/source, receipt, outbox or consumed document number may survive this pre-allocation validation refusal. Previously committed controls remain intact. An already saved valid draft is tested separately and is not described as a new failed request's surviving journal.
- **S5 — VOID IS STATE CHANGE, NOT DELETION:** An authorized void makes the posted entry absent from `active_general_ledger` and present in `voided_entries`; original source, journal, lines, posting audit and allocation identity remain byte-for-byte retained. The append-only void record and void audit commit atomically with reconciled plain-journal effects. Attempts to mutate, physically delete, unvoid or void the terminal entry again with a new request are refused by specific errors, with permitted controls; same-key retry returns the accepted receipt without a new mutation. No role names or future fine-grained void-visibility eligibility are encoded in tables/views/policies; preserve separate delete and module-read authority.
- **S6 — COMPLETE LIFECYCLE AUDIT:** For create → post → void, assert exactly one canonical lifecycle audit row per mutation, correct tenant, verified actor and truthful origin, with no secret material in before/after; separately assert the exact complete 041/042 physical change-event inventory per mutation, once per covered row change, with no missing or duplicate row events. Read the evidence through the authorized application audit path and independently verify stored evidence. Include a rollback failure probe proving business mutation and its success audit are atomic, plus positive read controls and sentinel checks. Define expected rows before observing results, never derive expectations by filtering whatever the implementation emitted.
- **S7 — IDEMPOTENCY:** Replay the identical post request with the same verified actor, tenant and idempotency key/digest, including a concurrent replay or lost-response retry. Assert one journal, one canonical posting audit row and exactly the original expected change-event set, one allocation/receipt/outbox effect and no added audit rows on replay. Same key with different content is refused by its specific idempotency-conflict error. The first permitted post is the positive control; session/tenant changes cannot inherit another actor's replay authority.
- **S8 — NUMBERING UNDER CONCURRENCY, D-016:** Two distinct concurrent permitted post requests receive distinct increasing document numbers via the scoped sequence and row-lock protocol, never MAX+1; both persist once. Prove actual overlapping requests with bounded barriers, not sequential await calls or an assumed timing race. Inject a failure after allocation: the number remains consumed/cancelled and is never reissued to either transaction or a different party; later success advances past it. Reapply/interruption probes retain or advance high-water state and refuse incompatible configuration. Gaps are expressly permitted and must never be back-filled; this is the later Principal override of the original gapless clause, not permission to weaken uniqueness, scope, concurrency or retained evidence.
- **S9 — NO TEST MAY PASS DISHONESTLY:** In particular for S3/S4/S6/S7, every denial assertion has a permitted-operation positive control; every refusal asserts its specific error class/code rather than generic failure/timeout/500/empty result. Expected audit and persistence counts use complete non-vacuous baselines. Delivery includes at least one actual mutation proof: remove a relevant guard in the disposable working copy, show the named permanent test fails for the expected assertion, restore it and show it passes; a comment predicting failure is insufficient, and the mutation must not be committed.
