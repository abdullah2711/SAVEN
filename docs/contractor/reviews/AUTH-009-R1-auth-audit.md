It deliberately serves enrollment and verification paths that can begin with insufficient assurance; calling it alone does not establish authorization for credential change.

Concrete source-derived ordering: a single-factor non-admin session passes the common gate while no active role requires MFA; an authority writer commits a new MFA-requiring membership or requires_mfa promotion before password-change mutation; the current-password request then passes assertMutationAuthority and changes the credential/revokes all sessions without the now-mandatory MFA. A factor lifecycle change clearing assurance between the common check and transaction also needs current-state refusal. The early check cannot reserve later authority. Reviewer has not executed these interleavings.

Acquire the accepted shared authority gate before principal/factor/session locks and authoritative reads for this mutation; re-read current required-MFA authority and current session/factor assurance after blocking acquisitions on the owned transaction and fail closed if unresolved or insufficient. Do not require a selected business tenant merely to change one's password, and do not borrow privileged operator context. Preserve immutable credential-generation advancement, success-audit atomicity and denied-attempt evidence.

Add real restricted-role controlled invalidation-first and mutation-first cases: membership insertion/role requires_mfa change before the final read must require MFA and leave password, epoch, revocations and success audit unchanged; a conflicting writer after final validation must wait until the credential transaction ends. Cover current assurance loss/factor lifecycle races and authorized MFA-positive/ordinary-user controls, exact error codes, bounded cleanup and no duplicate attempt counting.
