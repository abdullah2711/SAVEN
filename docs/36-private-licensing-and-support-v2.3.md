# Private composition, monthly certificates and support

Private customer composition: ERP frontend/API, local identity, tenant-local roles and business administration, SQLite data, local objects/workers/backup/diagnostics, public-key licence verifier and support desk. It excludes vendor commercial console, issuer/private signing key, global billing ledger, other subscribers and universal support credentials. A private runtime cannot grant extra commercial capabilities by changing a browser field.

SAVEN issuer signs versioned canonical claims: installation/subscriber IDs and installation-key digest; modules/edition/seats/company/offline limits; AI named seats/budgets; build/protocol bounds; not-before/valid-until; issuance sequence; key/algorithm/policy/grace references; support and overrides. Monthly renewal is required; the exact monthly cutoff and grace are not approved. Development examples use explicitly labelled fixture dates, not a commercial default.

Offline protocol: installation signs a fresh challenge; issuer verifies installation/entitlement and signs certificate; private verifier checks trusted public key, signature, installation/key binding, monotonic sequence and validity; installation emits a signed acceptance receipt binding challenge and certificate digest. Receipt proves an observed acceptance, not perpetual integrity or non-use after termination. Employees and password verifiers remain local; no automatic roster export. Active ERP assignments and named AI seats are distinct limits.

States: ACTIVE, RENEWAL_DUE, GRACE, RESTRICTED_FOR_NEW_OPERATIONS, CANCELLED_WITH_DATA_ACCESS, REVOKED_FOR_COMPROMISE, VERIFICATION_ERROR. Pending commercial terms are explicit. Missing/invalid/expired authority cannot create new posting rights; authorized historical read/export/backup/recovery remains available without deleting data, keys or pending offline evidence. No hidden backdoor or self-destruct path.

Support ticket is not access authority. Customer explicitly approves named staff, tenant/company, actions, duration and scope with MFA, local enforcement, notification, audit and revocation. Air-gapped evidence exchange/supervised service is supported by contract; outbound bridge is optional and off by default. High-risk changes require additional protected review. Root and hypervisor administrators can tamper or clone; detection/contract controls are not absolute prevention.

Acceptance: forged/mutated certificate, wrong installation/key, replayed sequence, expired certificate, rollback, key loss/recovery, offline import/receipt, clone limitations, seat ceiling, restore/reactivation and package scan proving issuer/global-commercial components are absent. Full private installation and commercial terms remain unapproved until independently tested.
